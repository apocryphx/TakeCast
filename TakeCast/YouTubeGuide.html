{{APP}}: YouTube setup guide

Record your event in 4K on your iPhone and stream it live to YouTube. Your full-quality copy is safe whatever happens to the stream.

1. What you need

  • A YouTube account with a verified channel (verify at youtube.com/verify).
  • Live streaming enabled on that channel.
  • Time: set this up at least a day before your event.
  • Someone else can do all of this for you. Give them the one-page insert at the end of this guide.

2. Enable live streaming

In YouTube Studio (studio.youtube.com) choose Create > Go live. The first time, YouTube asks you to enable live streaming.

YouTube can take up to 24 hours to enable live streaming the first time. Do this at least a day before your event.

YouTube states this wait for streaming from its mobile app. It is not confirmed for streaming from an encoder such as {{APP}}, but plan for it: missing it ruins an event.

3. Create the stream

  1. In the live control room, open Stream.
  2. Create a new stream key and choose HLS as its streaming protocol.
  3. In the stream settings, turn on Auto-start and Auto-stop. Then REC in {{APP}} starts the broadcast and STOP ends it, with no clicks in Studio during the event.
  4. Set visibility to Unlisted for the event (or Private for a test).
  5. Your ingest URL is built from the stream key:
    https://a.upload.youtube.com/http_upload_hls?cid=YOUR-STREAM-KEY&copy=0&file=

The ingest URL contains your stream key. Treat it like a password: anyone who has it can broadcast on your channel.

4. Paste the URL into {{APP}}

Open {{APP}} > Setup, turn on Stream to YouTube and paste the URL. Setup shows only the host (for example a.upload.youtube.com), never the key. Leave streaming off to record without streaming.

5. Test before the event

Run a short private test with a separate stream key: press REC, watch the LIVE lamp turn green, check the picture in Studio, press STOP. Do it at the venue if you can, where the phone will actually stand.

6. Share the right link

Send guests your channel's live link, youtube.com/@yourchannel/live. It always points to whatever is live now, so it keeps working even if a broadcast has to restart. A link to one broadcast does not.

7. During the event

  • Power: plug the phone in. 4K recording and streaming drain the battery fast.
  • Brightness: turn the screen well down. Heat lowers the stream before anything else.
  • Network: if the venue Wi-Fi is weak, turn Wi-Fi off. iPhone prefers any Wi-Fi over cellular, even a bad one.
  • Silence: turn on a Focus that allows no calls and no repeated calls, check alarms in Clock, and turn off Listen for "Siri" and vibration in Silent mode.
  • Stay in the app: leaving it pauses capture. Guided Access (Settings > Accessibility) keeps it on screen.
  • Frame the shot, tap LOCK, then press REC. At the end, keep recording a little past the moment before STOP.

8. After the event

Your full-quality 4K recording is on the phone. Open {{APP}} > Recordings and use Share to save it to Photos, Files, AirDrop or another app. An hour of 4K takes about 18 GB. The YouTube copy of the broadcast stays on your channel.

9. What the lamps mean

LampMeaning
RECTime and file size of the recording. REC INTERRUPTED (yellow): capture paused, usually because the app left the screen. Return to the app at once.
LIVESeconds since YouTube last accepted video. Green is healthy, orange is slow, red means the stream is struggling. The recording continues either way.
THERMPhone temperature. When it rises the stream is lowered or stopped to protect the recording.
BCASTThe stream's current quality (1080p, 720p or off) and what lowered it: therm (heat) or net (network).
DOCKA DockKit gimbal: battery and whether it is tracking (TRK) or holding still (HELD).

10. Good to know

  • Unlisted is not private. Anyone with the link can watch. Do not use it for confidential material.
  • Do not stream or record unpublished data, patient information, or anything covered by confidentiality rules or an institution's recording policy. Some institutions block YouTube.
  • Tell people in the room that they are being recorded.
  • For children's events, keep the stream unlisted and share the link only with the intended viewers.

This guide is practical advice, not legal advice.

For your support staff

Someone will record and stream an event from an iPhone with {{APP}}. This page is what they need from you.

YouTube can take up to 24 hours to enable live streaming the first time. Please start at least a day before the event.

Please create

  1. A verified YouTube channel with live streaming enabled (YouTube Studio > Create > Go live).
  2. A new stream key with the HLS streaming protocol.
  3. Stream settings: Auto-start and Auto-stop on, visibility Unlisted.

Please send the host

  1. The ingest URL: https://a.upload.youtube.com/http_upload_hls?cid=STREAM-KEY&copy=0&file=
  2. The channel's live link for guests: youtube.com/@channel/live

Send it safely

  • The ingest URL is a credential. Send it by AirDrop, a password manager or a direct message, never in a group chat, a public channel or a shared email thread.
  • Rotate (replace) the stream key after the event.
  • If you can, run a short private test stream with the host before the day.

Cautions

  • An unlisted link is not secure: anyone with it can watch.
  • Check the event against your institution's recording and confidentiality rules.
