# TakeCast privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-24.

TakeCast collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising and no tracking, and the developer runs no server that receives
anything from the app.

- **Camera and microphone.** Used only while you record. Recordings are saved
  in the app's own storage on your iPhone and leave it only when you share one
  yourself (for example to Photos, Files or AirDrop through the system share
  sheet).
- **Live stream.** When you paste a YouTube ingest URL and turn streaming on,
  video and audio are sent directly from your iPhone to that address, which
  belongs to YouTube (Google). What happens to the stream there is governed by
  YouTube's terms and Google's privacy policy. TakeCast sends nothing anywhere
  when streaming is off.
- **Settings and logs.** Your settings, including the ingest URL, are stored on
  your iPhone only. The app keeps a diagnostic log in its Documents folder,
  visible in the Files app. It never contains the ingest URL or stream key and
  is never sent anywhere by the app.
- **Gimbals.** DockKit accessory status (docked, battery, tracking) is read on
  the device to control the gimbal and is not stored or sent.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page. Do not include logs,
ingest URLs or stream keys.
